About Maker

Hello Fellow Hackers and Networkers! I am SirYanni, and I made Tappy to help avoid (imo pretty expensive)
NFC-Businesscard services. To use it, simply click "Create Contact" and enter your info.
When you're done, simply click "export" and your HTML and vcf file will be downloaded.
Then, you can just unzip the files and transfer them to your NFC card via your Phone.
